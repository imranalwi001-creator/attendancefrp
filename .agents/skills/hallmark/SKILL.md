# Hallmark — Anti-AI-Slop & Human-Craft Design Standard

A design skill for AI coding assistants. Makes the UIs they generate look made by human design craft, not generic AI slop.

## Core Rules for Mobile PWA & HRM Dashboards

1. **Eliminate Verbal Bloat (Minimal Words, Maximum Clarity)**:
   - No robotic explanations, verbose disclaimers, or multi-paragraph labels.
   - Clean micro-copy: "Presensi Hari Ini", "Wajah terdeteksi. Silakan berpose ✔", "Ambil Foto untuk Absen".
   - Information scannable in < 2 seconds.

2. **Zero AI-Slop Colors & Gradients**:
   - Strictly avoid generic AI violet/purple gradients on everything.
   - Use intentional, harmonic color systems: tailored emerald/forest-teal (`#165B4C` -> `#2B9B82`), crisp slate borders (`#E2E8F0`), and pure white surfaces with subtle tactile shadows.

3. **Cognitive Ergonomics & High Contrast**:
   - Minimum 48px touch targets for mobile thumb zones.
   - High visual contrast (WCAG AAA compliant text).
   - Honest data: real employee records, real shift hours, real GPS meters, no fabricated placeholder metrics.

4. **Component State Discipline**:
   - Every button and interactive control must have distinct tactile feedback (default, active press, disabled, loading).
   - 60fps GPU-accelerated micro-transitions (`transform: scale(0.98)` on tap).
